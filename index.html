I · The beginning

One day.
One street.
A beginning.

It began on 31 July 2019, in the book-lined lanes of College Street. We had both gone book-shopping with our common friends, unaware that an ordinary outing would become the first page of a story of our own.

What followed began with phone conversations, helping each other with studies, and the little moments through which two people gradually became an important part of each other's lives.

31 July 2019 · College Street, Kolkata
II · Words × Personalities

Different
beginnings.
One journey.

Two distinct personalities, two ways of seeing the world—and a life to share.

The groom

Deep
Sarkar

Practical and reserved, Deep is more of a listener than a talker. He finds joy in long bike rides, getting lost in a good book, and watching films. Curious by nature, he enjoys exploring life at his own thoughtful pace.

The bride

Disha
Ghosh

Talkative, expressive and wonderfully impulsive, Disha brings lively energy to everyday moments. She loves cooking exquisite meals, discovering different kinds of food, and expressing herself through dancing and singing.

III · The balance

Different notes,
one beautiful melody.

One is practical and reserved; the other is spontaneous and talkative. Deep and Disha may approach life differently, but those differences are part of what makes their bond their own. Between thoughtful plans and unexpected ideas, quiet moments and endless conversations, they continue to find their rhythm together.
